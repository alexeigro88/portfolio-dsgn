TrueScan

An operations platform for a company that 3D-scans buildings and construction sites, designed from scratch: one system from the first request to a scanned, staffed and costed project.

TrueScan captures existing buildings with laser scanners, drones and Matterport and turns the data into models and drawings. The work runs along one long chain: a request becomes a deal, the deal needs a detailed reality-capture proposal, a won deal becomes a site project with a crew, and field hours, per diem and mileage add up to the project’s cost. I built the product around that chain: the information architecture, a design system and five modules ready for development.

How the platform works

One record travels through five modules. Data entered once moves forward: a converted lead becomes a deal, the deal fills the proposal, a won deal opens the project, and approved time becomes its cost.

Problems

A scanning company sells, plans and bills complex field work. Every step had its own tool, or none.

01

Requests come from everywhere

Web forms, direct traffic and each sales rep’s personal form. A lead needs an owner and a status fast, or it goes cold.

02

Every deal needs a heavy proposal

A reality-capture proposal has 18 sections: scope, services, team, equipment, terms and fees that depend on what is being scanned.

03

Site work is scattered

After a deal is won, the building data, the crew, the files and multi-gigabyte point clouds need one home.

04

Costs live in a spreadsheet

Field hours, per diem and mileage were logged in Google Sheets, so the real cost of a project was hard to see.

Five modules

Each module answers one question for one role, and hands its data to the next.

01 · Leads

Triage in seconds

  • Table and Kanban with status tabs and live counts
  • Owner assigned in one click, status changed from the row
  • Convert to opportunity only when a lead is Qualified
02 · Opportunities

A pipeline that shows the next step

  • Stages in pipeline order, from Prospecting to Won
  • Close dates turn amber within 7 days and red when overdue; deals idle for 30 days are flagged
  • Each card suggests the next step: add line items or create a proposal
03 · Proposals

An 18-section proposal, built from the deal

  • Client, scope and line items come from the opportunity
  • Scopes like drone capture, Matterport or modeling switch their sections and fees on and off
  • A readiness check before sending; a sent revision is locked
04 · Projects

Everything about a site in one place

  • Created when a deal is won; site data and the signed proposal carry over
  • Overview, Team, Billing, Documents, Board and Notes
  • Cost to date from approved time; billing visible only to the right roles
05 · Time tracking

Field time that becomes project cost

  • A weekly timesheet modeled on the team’s spreadsheet
  • Client projects and internal work in one place, approved by week
  • Labor = hours × rate, plus per diem and mileage at the project’s rates

Details that matter

Small decisions that save clicks every day: show consequences before they happen, apply small changes instantly, always leave a way back.

01

Preview before converting

Conversion shows exactly what will be created — a new or existing account, a contact and an opportunity in Prospecting — with no fields to fill in.

02

Land on the result

After conversion the user lands on the new opportunity, with a one-time “Converted from lead” banner and a link back to the lead.

03

Small changes apply instantly

Small changes apply instantly: assigning an owner or changing a status needs no Apply button, and archiving comes with an 8-second Undo.

04

Guided entry

A 3-step wizard validates one step at a time, checks duplicate emails when leaving the field and fills the address from a suggestion.

Design system

A light canvas, a dark sidebar and one orange call to action per screen. Moderate radii and a single shadow per element, in line with the TrueScan website.
