Uncloud360

A private AI coach for work, emotions and life. Talk it through in one tap and leave with one clear next step.

People open a coaching app when something is heavy: burnout at work, tension at home, a decision they keep putting off. In that moment the interface has to get out of the way. The task was a calm, clear product where a conversation with the assistant is always one tap away, a mascot that makes the AI feel approachable without being childish, and a visual style built on the Uncloud 360 brand book.

The core flow

Open the app, tap AI Voice, talk, and get a summary with one next step. Typing is always there as an alternative.

Principles

Four rules that shaped every screen.

Lightness through air

Soft mist gradients, hairline borders and generous spacing instead of glows, glass or dark slabs.

Warm, not childish

A friendly character for the key moments, words for mood instead of emojis, one calm typeface.

One clear action

Each screen has one primary button in Teal. AI Voice is the hero of Home and the centre of the tab bar.

Built for the thumb

Bottom tab bar, 44 pt+ targets, primary actions in the lower half of the screen.

Key screens

Each one answers a single question: what do I do now?

Home

One hero action

  • “Talk it through” starts a voice session in one tap
  • Two quick actions: guided paths and live coaching
  • A one-word mood check-in and what’s up next
AI Voice

The mascot is the interface

  • The character is the centre of the screen, with a clear state: listening, thinking or speaking
  • A large live transcript under it
  • Pause, captions and end within thumb reach
Session summary

Nothing is lost

  • An insight in plain words and the topics it touched
  • One next step that can be added to the plan
  • Key moments with timestamps, saved to the journal
Journal

One private feed

  • Notes, session summaries and check-ins together
  • A daily prompt to start writing
  • Search and filters by type
Crisis resources

Safety comes first

  • A clear note that this is AI coaching, not therapy or emergency care
  • Free 24/7 lines with one-tap call or text
  • Reachable from Profile → Support at any time

The mascot

A friendly guide with a job to do. The soft 3D cloud character welcomes people, points to the next action and presents insights, so the AI feels approachable from the very first screen.

Beyond the session

Journal, guided paths, crisis resources and a profile that groups plan, coaching and personal settings.

Design system

Built on the Uncloud 360 brand book. Navy carries text and structure, Teal is reserved for the one primary action and AI Voice, Mist creates air between blocks.
