BookKeeper AI

An internal assistant for an accounting firm that answers bookkeepers only from published, validated SOPs — and shows the source behind every answer.

Bookkeepers work across many clients, each with its own close process, platforms and exceptions. The answers live in SOPs, videos and walkthroughs of varying freshness. A generic chatbot would guess — in accounting a confident wrong answer costs more than no answer. So the product is built around trust: scope, sources, status and a human review loop.

Problems

The knowledge exists, but it’s hard to trust: scattered, aging and tied to specific clients.

01

SOPs go stale. Platforms and policies change, and nobody knows which guide is still valid.

02

Every client has its own workflow. Mixing up two clients’ SOPs is a real risk.

03

When there’s no documented answer, people improvise — and the gap stays invisible.

04

Subject-matter experts are a bottleneck: validation requests pile up with no clear priorities.

Solutions

Make every answer traceable, scoped to the right client and backed by people who own the knowledge.

01

Answers come only from published sources, with citations, provenance and the source’s lifecycle and certification status.

02

Before answering, the assistant clarifies scope: general guidance or a specific client. Another client’s SOPs can’t appear in the answer.

03

Compliance-sensitive answers backed by a source that needs revalidation are shown only after an explicit acknowledgment.

04

Unanswered questions are logged and grouped into knowledge gaps; SMEs and QC get a prioritized overview of what needs attention.
